Hyperborea. Revista de ensayo y creación wants its website to be usable without barriers: with a keyboard, with a screen reader, with text or screen magnification and with high-contrast modes. This statement says what we do, what we know still falls short, and how to tell us about a barrier.

## Conformance status

The goal is to meet the [Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) at level AA. **Current status: partially conformant.** Automated checks run on every change and currently find no failures; the manual evaluation with screen readers and other assistive technologies is still to be completed, and content imported from the previous site has the limitations listed below.

## What we do

- **Tests on every change.** Before any change is published, the following run automatically: the axe-core tool with the WCAG 2.0, 2.1 and 2.2 rules (levels A and AA) plus best practices on a fixed sample of twenty pages in the three languages, HTML validation of every page, and structure checks: a single main heading, alternative text on every image, a declared language, and a “Skip to main content” link as the first element.
- **Keyboard.** Everything can be used with the keyboard. The focus is always visible and the fixed header never covers it. The submenu opens with Enter or Space and closes with Esc.
- **Contrast and colour.** Text and control borders meet the 4.5:1 and 3:1 contrast ratios. Links inside text are underlined and not told apart by colour alone.
- **Adaptability.** Content reflows down to 320 pixels wide without horizontal scrolling, tolerates increased text spacing, respects the reduced-motion preference and Windows forced high-contrast mode.
- **Languages.** Every text declares its language. Texts that exist only in Spanish are shown as such in the English and French versions, with their language marked, so that screen readers pronounce them correctly.

## Known limitations

- **Images inside texts.** The papers imported from the previous site contain 258 images in the body text. All have a description, but 43 have a generic one such as “Figure 4”, which identifies the image without describing it.
- **Featured images.** They are treated as decorative, because the title is right next to them, unless the editors provide a description.
- **PDF files.** Many papers are also offered as PDF. We do not guarantee that these files are tagged or that their text can be selected: some may be scans. The full text is always on the page itself.
- **Logo.** The logo in the header is a low-resolution image and looks blurry on high-density screens. We plan to replace it with a vector version.
- **Manual evaluation pending.** We have not yet completed tests with screen readers (such as VoiceOver, NVDA or TalkBack) or with magnifiers and dictation software.

## How to tell us about a barrier

If something cannot be read or used, write to us at [admin@microagencia.com](mailto:admin@microagencia.com). Please give the address of the page, describe the problem you found and, if you wish, the assistive technology you use. We will reply as soon as possible and, if the content cannot be fixed right away, offer you an alternative.

## Date and review

This statement was prepared on 21 November 2026. We will review it at least once a year and whenever the site changes significantly.
